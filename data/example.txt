Between Thought and Becoming

We think,
and somewhere in the silence,
a world begins to breathe.

No footsteps,
no hands,
no sound—
yet mountains rise
inside the mind.

We imagine a door
before we ever touch its handle,
a road
before our feet know where to walk.

Perhaps that is the quiet magic—
that the mind can visit places
the body has never seen.

A thought becomes a picture,
a picture becomes a feeling,
a feeling becomes a desire,
and slowly,
desire becomes direction.

But thoughts are strange creatures.
They can build kingdoms
or invisible cages.

So learn to watch them.

Let them arrive.
Let them pass.

For you are not every thought
that crosses your mind—
you are the one
who can choose
which thought deserves
to become a life.

And maybe,
just maybe,
creation begins
long before the world can see it—

in that tiny, silent moment

when you close your eyes

and dare to imagine
what does not yet exist.
